02 / THE SURPRISE

Same streets.
Different engines.
One living flow.

A 2026 reconstruction led by Natalia Mrnjavac suggests the last universal common ancestor encoded enzymes for only about half of a 420-reaction network. Geological metals may have catalysed much of the rest.

Bacterial and archaeal ancestors later appear to have evolved structurally different enzymes for some equivalent jobs. That is one ancient network becoming self-sufficient along two paths - not two separate origins of life.
