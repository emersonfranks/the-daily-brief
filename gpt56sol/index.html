02 / What the model earned

Pruning did not save everything. Across six fixed runs, it left a smaller network whose surviving nodes were healthier and less uneven after stress.

This is a mathematical analogy. The ecosystem paper and circuit-breaker practice are not evidence of one shared physical cause. This page applies one stylized stress-network model to both vocabularies and reports only what that model produced.

What would change the conclusion? Any shipped seed where pruning fails to improve survivor health, fails to reduce dispersion, leaves no remnant, or preserves capability without cost.

03 / Where the idea came from

Primary research · 14 Aug 2026

Extinction drives emergent metastability in complex ecosystems

Park, Rogers and Baron model demographic fluctuations pruning low-abundance species, leaving lower-diversity remnant communities with robust metastability.

 Independent technical source · updated 2 Jul 2026

Circuit Breaker Pattern

Microsoft Architecture Center describes blocking calls to likely-failing services to prevent cascades, preserve stability, and deliberately degrade functionality.
Open the mechanism +
1. Stress accumulates

A pulse lowers every node’s health. A struggling neighbor adds dependency stress through each connecting edge.

2. A threshold removes interaction

With isolation on, a node below 0.17 is removed. In the two readings that means extinction or an open breaker.

3. The remnant pays less spillover

Removed nodes no longer transmit their deficit. Health and dispersion are measured over the surviving core.

4. Stability is not free

The same removal reduces active-node fraction. A calmer graph with fewer capabilities is graceful degradation, not recovery.
