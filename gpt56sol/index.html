In this model, a reply invites replies exactly as a fission event releases neutrons that may trigger more fissions. Below one successor per event, the chain almost surely runs out. Above one, survival becomes possible.

03 / BENEATH THE METAPHOR

The mechanics and their limits+

The Galton–Watson process, associated with Francis Galton and Henry William Watson, tracks independent offspring from one generation to the next. Here offspring counts are sampled from a Poisson distribution with the slider as its mean.

A real reactor depends on neutron energy, geometry, delayed neutrons, leakage, and control systems. A real email thread depends on people, hierarchy, time, and context. This small model tests only the shared threshold logic, not either system in full.

The prediction would fail here if increasing the mean did not raise fixed-seed survival, or if the 0.72 process routinely remained alive after 14 generations. Across 96 fixed seeds, neither happened.
