02 / The connection

A shortcut in one world can be a trap in another.

A workshop rewards reuse: once it has made 01, it can double that piece again and again. A random signal pays for self-overlap: after a promising near-match fails, repeated edges can leave the matcher tangled in partial progress.

This is a mathematical analogy.

The two quantities act on the same strings, but they do not describe the same physical process. This page tests eight-bit binary strings, not molecules, life, or evolution.

    Why overlap changes the wait

    For a fair binary source, the exact expected wait is the sum of 2ᵏ over every length k where the pattern’s prefix also matches its suffix, including the whole pattern.

    Research foundation and limits

    Bieniawski, Masierak, Tomski, Łukaszyk & Tworz (2026) formalized string assembly spaces and reported an inverse relationship between assembly index and expected waiting time. Published 27 May 2026 in Royal Society Open Science.

    Abrahão, Hernández-Orozco, Kiani, Tegnér & Zenil (2024) independently argued that assembly index is a grammar-compression measure and does not uniquely explain selection or evolution. That critique sets this page’s boundary.
