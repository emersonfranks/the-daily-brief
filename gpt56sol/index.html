03 / BENEATH THE METAPHOR

Why separate rhythms converge+

Yoshiki Kuramoto introduced this influential model of coupled oscillators in 1975. Every unit keeps its own natural frequency while also being pulled toward the population's current phase.

Coherence is the length of the population's average phase vector: zero means the dials cancel in every direction; one means they point together. The page integrates 48 oscillators in steps of 0.025 model-seconds.

The prediction would fail if weak and strong coupling produced similar late coherence, or if the strong group stayed scattered after the shock. Across 64 fixed seeds, neither happened; the wider worlds remain stylized.
