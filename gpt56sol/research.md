## External-research shortlist

### 1. Two routes from geochemistry to autonomous metabolism

- **Discipline:** origin-of-life biochemistry / evolutionary biology
- **Source:** "Intermediate stages in the origin of metabolism at a
  phosphorylating hydrothermal vent," Natalia Mrnjavac et al., *Science
  Advances*
- **Verified date:** 5 August 2026
- **URL:** <https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aef3128>
- **Finding:** A reconstructed 420-reaction network suggests LUCA encoded
  enzymes for only about half the reactions. Environmental metals could have
  catalysed much of the rest; bacterial and archaeal lineages later evolved
  structurally different enzymes for some equivalent jobs. The paper also
  demonstrates phosphite/palladium-driven phosphorylation as a possible
  pre-ATP mechanism.
- **Interactive possibility:** a settlement gradually replaces shared,
  landscape-provided utilities with two independently invented sets of local
  machines while preserving the same flow network.

### 2. A maturation gate suppresses human axon regrowth

- **Discipline:** developmental neuroscience / regenerative medicine
- **Source:** "A human corticospinal organoid-slice connectoid model informs
  enhancer strategies for post-injury axon regrowth," George M. Gibbons et
  al., *Cell Reports*
- **Verified date:** 26 May 2026
- **URL:** <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117399>
- **Finding:** A connected human cortical-organoid/spinal-slice model regrew
  injured axons robustly before roughly culture day 150, then sharply less as
  a maturation-linked regulatory network switched on. Genetic interference
  and lynestrenol increased regrowth in mature cultures. This is an organoid
  proof of concept, not a treatment result in people.
- **Interactive possibility:** a city's road-repair permits expire as it
  matures; selectively reopening a permit restores bridge construction without
  guaranteeing that every bridge reaches the right destination.

### 3. Molecular handedness identified meteorite contamination

- **Discipline:** astrobiology / analytical organic chemistry
- **Source:** "Racemic isoprenoids in the Murchison meteorite derive from
  petroleum-based aerosol pollutants," Guillaume Leseigneur et al., *Earth
  and Planetary Science Letters*
- **Verified date:** 29 May 2026
- **URL:** <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsl.2026.120141>
- **Finding:** ExoMars-MOMA-like columns separated the handed forms of pristane
  and phytane. Their approximately equal left/right proportions, together with
  oil-shale comparisons, support terrestrial petroleum aerosol contamination
  rather than fresh biological provenance.
- **Interactive possibility:** infer where suitcase contents came from by
  sorting left and right gloves through a timed checkpoint.

### 4. Phosphate scarcity can govern open-ocean methane production

- **Discipline:** biological oceanography / climate biogeochemistry
- **Source:** "Phosphate scarcity governs methane production in the global
  open ocean," Shengyu Wang, Hairong Xu and Thomas S. Weber, *PNAS*
- **Verified date:** 17 March 2026
- **URL:** <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2521235123>
- **Finding:** A data-assimilating global model reproduced observed methane
  supersaturation when production was linked to phosphate scarcity,
  particularly methylphosphonate cleavage in subtropical gyres. Its future
  increase is a model projection, not an observed doubling.
- **Interactive possibility:** an office tower opens phosphorus-bearing
  emergency parcels when ordinary deliveries fail, releasing methane-like
  packing waste that feeds back on the building's temperature.

### 5. Hidden order can swing while total brightness barely changes

- **Discipline:** astrophysics / black-hole plasma physics
- **Source:** "Full-polarization millimeter wavelength variability of
  Sagittarius A* during the 2018 EHT campaign," Ezequiel Albentosa-Ruiz et al.
  with the Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration
- **Verified date:** 11 April 2026
- **URL:** <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10287>
- **Finding:** ALMA observations varied by less than 10% in total millimetre
  intensity while linear and circular polarization varied by about 30% and
  50%. A coherent polarization loop during the 24 April flare challenges a
  simple cooling-only account and favours continued energy injection.
- **Interactive possibility:** a stadium's total glow stays stable while the
  orientations of thousands of glow sticks rotate into a changing hidden
  pattern.

### 7. A shallower Cascadia slab changes a local shaking estimate

## Primary and independent verification for the leading candidate

**Primary paper.** Natalia Mrnjavac et al., "Intermediate stages in the origin
of metabolism at a phosphorylating hydrothermal vent," *Science Advances*,
published 5 August 2026.
<https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aef3128>. The paper's network reconstruction,
protein structures, gene histories and phosphorylation experiment support a
transition in which geological catalysts did jobs that independently evolved
enzymes later took over in bacterial and archaeal ancestry.

**Independent report.** Gracy Narayan, "One genetic code, two paths to
life-German study offers new clues to bacteria, archaea," *ThePrint*, 13
August 2026.
<https://theprint.in/science/origin-of-life-genetic-code-two-paths-german-study-bacteria-archaea/3013708/>.
The report independently describes LUCA's partial enzyme repertoire, the
separate bacterial and archaeal enzyme solutions, and the
phosphite/palladium-driven phosphorylation result. It also guards the key
interpretive boundary: this is not evidence for two unrelated origins of life.

## Provisional prediction

If a reaction network is viable when shared environmental catalysts serve
every node, then progressively replacing those catalysts with either of two
independent enzyme toolkits should preserve throughput once each essential
reaction is covered. The two toolkits may share function while sharing little
machinery.

**Falsifier:** if either complete toolkit cannot sustain every required flow,
or if replacing environmental catalysts necessarily lowers steady-state
throughput, the proposed "same network, different machinery" thesis fails.

The final selection remains provisional until the topic-ledger collision check
and headless measurements are complete.

## Ledger check and final selection

The ledger was checked after the shortlist was saved. None of its pairings
combines hydrothermal-vent-supported metabolism with a settlement replacing
shared utilities, and none merely renames that mechanism. The nearest network
entries concern routing, cascade failure or synchronization rather than a
transfer of catalytic function from landscape to independently evolved local
machinery.

I selected candidate 1 because it offers a controllable causal structure,
strong recent primary and independent sources, and an important boundary the
page can make visible: two toolkits can take over the same jobs without
implying two origins of life. The final pairing is **vent-supported chemistry
becoming autonomous metabolism** and **a company town replacing shared
utilities with local machines**. The analogy is a deliberately stylized
mathematical mapping, not an empirical claim about towns.
