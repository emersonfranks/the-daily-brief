# Research Shortlist — 2026-08-28

## Candidate Survey (5 Candidates across 5 Disciplines)

### 1. Active Matter / Non-Equilibrium Statistical Physics
- **Title:** Multiple pattern formation in quorum sensing of density enhanced motility
- **Authors:** Itay Azizi
- **Verified Date:** 2026-08-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25324
- **Finding:** In density-enhanced motility (DEM) where particles are passive below critical density $\rho_c$ and active above it, the system undergoes inverse quorum-sensing phase separation, forming breathing cluster rings, dynamic blastwaves, and labyrinthine microphases.
- **Potential Pairing:** Microbial quorum blastwaves $\leftrightarrow$ Self-regulated starbursts in molecular clouds (Jeans collapse triggering radiative feedback dispersal).

### 2. Computational Neuroscience / Network Dynamics
- **Title:** Hysteresis and multistability in network spreading with neuronal activity feedback
- **Authors:** Christoffer G. Alexandersen, Dani S. Bassett
- **Verified Date:** 2026-08-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26528
- **Finding:** When spreading processes on complex networks modulate node activity, feedback creates explosive transitions, bistability, and hysteresis loops.
- **Potential Pairing:** Prion/pathology propagation $\leftrightarrow$ Cascading power grid failure under load shedding.

### 3. Granular Physics / Collective Robotics
- **Title:** Emergence of Stigmergic Transport in Granular Environments
- **Authors:** F. Wéry, F. N. Pinan Basualdo, B. Gorissen
- **Verified Date:** 2026-08-25
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24383
- **Finding:** Environmental memory (physical track depression in granular substrates) coupled with geometrical crowding spontaneously organizes random walkers into braided stigmergic highways.
- **Potential Pairing:** Robot granular track formation $\leftrightarrow$ Glacial meltwater braided river network channeling.

### 4. Fluid Dynamics / Turbulence
- **Title:** Dynamical slowdown, bottlenecks, and multiscaling in Voigt-regularised turbulence
- **Authors:** Anikat Kankaria, Bikram Pal, Edriss S. Titi
- **Verified Date:** 2026-08-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27355
- **Finding:** Scale-dependent regularisation induces intermediate wavenumber bottleneck accumulations of kinetic energy before viscous dissipation cascades.
- **Potential Pairing:** Turbulent kinetic bottlenecking $\leftrightarrow$ Supply chain buffer inventory accumulation under lead-time variance.

### 5. Probability Theory / Lattice Statistical Mechanics
- **Title:** Cutoff with an O(1) window for Potts Glauber Dynamics on lattice at High Temperature
- **Authors:** Seoyeon Yang, Allan Sly
- **Verified Date:** 2026-08-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26259
- **Finding:** Glauber dynamics on $q$-state Potts lattices exhibits a sharp mixing time cutoff with an $O(1)$ temporal transition window at high temperatures.
- **Potential Pairing:** Potts lattice spin thermalization $\leftrightarrow$ Consensus formation in multi-party election polling shocks.
